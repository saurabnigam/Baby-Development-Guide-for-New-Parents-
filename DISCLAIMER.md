# Important Notice — Please Read

## This is parent education, not medical advice

This guide is written to help you understand child development and to give you practical things to try. It is **not** a substitute for care from a qualified professional who knows your child.

**Nothing here is a diagnosis, a treatment plan, or a reason to delay getting help.**

Your paediatrician, family doctor, midwife, health visitor, nurse, or therapist knows things about your child that no written guide can: their history, their growth, their hearing and vision, their family circumstances, and how they actually present in a room. When this guide and your clinician disagree, **follow your clinician.**

## Get professional advice before changing anything if your child

- was born prematurely (and use **corrected age** for milestones, not birth age)
- had a low birth weight, or has growth concerns
- has any diagnosed medical condition, disability, or developmental diagnosis
- has feeding difficulties, reflux, allergies, or a strong family history of allergy
- has a heart, breathing, neurological, or muscular condition
- takes any regular medication
- has known or suspected hearing or vision problems

## Don't wait and see

The single most common regret parents report is having waited. If you are concerned about your child's development, hearing, vision, feeding, growth, behaviour, or mood:

**Ask now.** An unnecessary appointment costs you an hour. A delayed one can cost intervention time that does not come back. Early support works better than late support across essentially every developmental domain.

**Trust your gut.** Parental concern is a genuinely useful clinical signal, and clinicians know it. "Something feels off and I can't say what" is a legitimate reason to book an appointment.

## Emergencies

This guide is never the right resource in an emergency. Call your local emergency number or go to your nearest emergency department for:

- difficulty breathing, choking, or blue/grey colour
- unresponsiveness, a seizure, or a serious head injury
- suspected poisoning or battery ingestion (**button/coin-cell batteries are a same-hour emergency**)
- any submersion incident followed by breathing difficulty
- severe allergic reaction — swelling, wheezing, collapse
- a baby under 3 months with a fever
- any situation where you think a child may be seriously unwell

**If a child or teenager talks about suicide or self-harm:** do not leave them alone, remove access to means, and get help the same day. **US/Canada: 988. UK & Ireland: 116 123. India: 14416.** Or your local emergency number. See [Module 14](14-age-12-18/README.md#if-you-are-worried-about-suicide-or-self-harm).

**If you are at the end of your rope with a crying baby:** put the baby down on their back in a safe cot and walk away for a few minutes. That is always safe. **Never shake a baby.** See [Module 03](03-newborn-0-3-months/README.md#read-this-first-when-the-crying-wont-stop).

## Regional differences

This guide draws primarily on **AAP (US)**, **CDC (US)**, and **WHO** guidance, with some UK and Indian context. Recommendations differ between countries on vitamin D dosing, weaning timing, milestone checklists, immunisation schedules, well-child visit timing, and the legal age for staying home alone.

Where a recommendation is country-specific, we've tried to say so. **Check what applies where you live.** Crisis numbers, emergency services, and early-intervention entitlements are all local.

## Sources and currency

Guidance changes. Content reflects widely accepted guidance at the time of writing, and where the evidence is contested or a popular claim is weaker than it sounds, we say so rather than smoothing it over — see [RED-TEAM-REVIEW.md](RED-TEAM-REVIEW.md) for the audit that produced several of those caveats.

If you find something here that is out of date or wrong, that is worth raising. Health content that isn't corrected gets worse over time.

## What this guide will not do

- It will not tell you there is one right way to parent
- It will not make you feel guilty
- It will not claim that a window has closed
- It will not sell you a product, a programme, or a method

Every activity here is a suggestion. Do what works for your child, your family, and your circumstances.
