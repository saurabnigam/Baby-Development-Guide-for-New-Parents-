## 📱 Card 3: Pediatric Supplement & MCT Powder Checklist

```text
💊 *Daily Supplements & Fortification Routine* 📋

1️⃣ *Hemfer Drops (Iron Supplement):*
• *Dose:* As prescribed by pediatrician
• *Timing:* Mid-morning between feeds (on an empty stomach with Vitamin C)
• ⚠️ *Rule:* Never give with milk/formula/calcium (calcium blocks iron absorption by 50–60%). Separate by at least 1 hour.
• 💡 *Tip:* Drop onto inner cheek (not front teeth); wipe gums/teeth with a damp washcloth to prevent harmless staining. Dark green/black poops are completely normal!

2️⃣ *Vitamin D Drops (800 IU / 2 Drops):*
• *Timing:* Give with morning or afternoon milk/food
• ⚠️ *Rule:* Vitamin D is fat-soluble; giving it with milk or ghee doubles absorption into the bloodstream for bone growth.

3️⃣ *MCT Powder (Calorie Fortification for Weight):*
• *Dose:* As prescribed by doctor (start with 0.5g–1.0g/feed and titrate)
• *How to Mix:* Stir into warm porridge, khichdi, or puree *right before serving*.
• ⚠️ *Rule:* Never boil MCT powder directly on the stove; do not give raw powder directly into mouth.
```

## 📱 Card 4: Quick 5-Minute High-Calorie Baby Growth Recipes

```text
🥑 *Top 3 High-Calorie Baby Growth Recipes (6+ Months)* 🍲

1. *Super-Fortified Moong Dal Khichdi:*
   • 2 tbsp yellow moong dal + 1 tbsp rice, cooked very soft
   • Mash thoroughly with fork
   • Stir in: 1 full tsp pure A2 Desi Ghee + pinch of roasted cumin
   • Add prescribed MCT powder right before feeding

2. *Sprouted Ragi & Almond Mash:*
   • 2 tbsp sprouted ragi flour cooked in water until glossy
   • Stir in: 1/2 tsp smooth almond butter + 1/2 tsp ghee
   • Top with a splash of breastmilk or formula for creaminess

3. *Butter-Scrambled Egg Yolk & Avocado:*
   • 1 hard-boiled egg yolk mashed with 1 tbsp ripe avocado
   • Add 1/2 tsp unsalted butter or olive oil
   • High in choline, healthy fats, and epiphyseal bone-building nutrients!
```

## 📱 Card 5: Safe Sleep & Soothing SOS (Harvey Karp 5 S's)

```text
🌙 *Fussy Evening / Meltdown Soothing (The 5 S's)* 🧸

When baby is overtired or crying inconsolably, trigger the *Calming Reflex*:

1. *Swaddle:* Wrap snugly with hands down (⚠️ stop swaddling completely once baby tries to roll!).
2. *Side or Stomach:* Hold baby on their side across your forearm while soothing (*ONLY in your arms awake, never asleep in a cot!*).
3. *Shush:* Loud white noise close to ear ("shhh-shhh" or sound machine at <65 dB).
4. *Swing:* Rhythmic, gentle bobbing motion supporting head and neck (never shake).
5. *Suck:* Offer clean pacifier, finger, or breast.

💤 *Safe Sleep Reminder:* Always Back to sleep, flat firm cot, zero pillows or blankets!
```

## 📱 Card 6: What to Ask the Pediatrician at the Next Checkup

```text
🩺 *Checkup Questions for Our Pediatrician* 📝

1. What is baby's current *Weight-for-Age* and *Length-for-Age* percentile on the WHO chart?
2. What is the *Weight-for-Length* symmetry?
3. What was our weight velocity (grams/day) over the last 30 days? (Target: ~15–20 g/day for 3–6m, ~12–15 g/day for 6–9m).
4. Shall we continue MCT powder at the current dose or adjust?
5. How long should we maintain the 800 IU Vitamin D therapeutic dose before stepping down to 400 IU?
6. Check hemoglobin / iron status.
```
