## How to use this

Four columns, matching the [Teaching Loop](../23-life-skills-critical-moments/README.md#part-1-the-teaching-loop):

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **I** | **Introduced** — I've modelled it and talked about it |
| **H** | **With help** — they do it, I'm hands-on or prompting each step |
| **A** | **Alone** — they do it independently when asked |
| **U** | **Unprompted** — they do it without being asked, reliably |

Mark the date, not a tick. `03/26` tells you far more than `✓`.

**Two things to remember:**

- The **age windows are typical, not targets.** A child two years behind a window on shoelaces is a child who hasn't practised shoelaces. It is not a signal about anything else.
- For safety skills, **being early is worse than being late.** A child who can physically do something is not necessarily a child with the judgement to do it unsupervised. Those are separate assessments.

## The Parent's Own Checklist

Not skills to teach — things to do. Review annually.

- [ ] Infant/child CPR and choking response — in-person class · *last done:* __________
- [ ] Basic first aid course · *last done:* __________
- [ ] Home safety sweep redone for current mobility stage · *last done:* __________
- [ ] Four-sided isolation pool fencing (if applicable), self-closing and self-latching
- [ ] Smoke alarms tested · *last done:* __________
- [ ] Fire escape plan practised with children · *last done:* __________
- [ ] Medicines, cleaning products, **button/coin-cell batteries** locked away
- [ ] Emergency and poison numbers written somewhere physical, not only in a phone
- [ ] Car seat correctly installed and correct for current height/weight · *last checked:* __________
- [ ] Firearms (if any) locked, ammunition stored separately
- [ ] Furniture and TVs anchored to walls
- [ ] Window locks/guards above ground floor; blind cords secured
- [ ] Child knows my phone number from memory · *verified:* __________
