# Product Requirements Document: Baby IQ & EQ Development Guide

## Vision

Empower parents with a comprehensive, science-backed guide to nurture their child's cognitive (IQ) and emotional (EQ) development from birth through adulthood, with deep focus on the critical 0–5 year window.

## Problem Statement

Parents are overwhelmed by conflicting advice about child development. Most resources are either too academic, too simplistic, or commercially motivated. There is no single, structured, evidence-based guide that walks parents through every developmental stage with clear milestones, actionable activities, nutritional guidance, and emotional support strategies.

## Target Audience

- **Primary**: First-time parents (prenatal through age 5)
- **Secondary**: Experienced parents wanting evidence-based refresh
- **Tertiary**: Grandparents, caregivers, early childhood educators

## Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Clarity score | Parents can explain any module's key concepts to another parent |
| Actionability | Every module contains ≥5 specific activities parents can do today |
| Scientific backing | Every major claim links to peer-reviewed research |
| Comprehensiveness | Covers birth → 18 years with 70% weight on 0–5 years |

## Course Structure

### Part 1: Foundation & Science (3 modules)
Understanding how baby brains work — the WHY behind everything.

- **00** Prerequisites & How to Use This Guide
- **01** Brain Science Fundamentals
- **02** Developmental Milestones Overview (IQ & EQ)

### Part 2: The Critical First Years — 0 to 2 (6 modules)
Deep-dive into every quarter of the most explosive growth period.

- **03** Newborn: 0–3 Months (Sensory Awakening)
- **04** Infant: 3–6 Months (Social Engagement)
- **05** Infant: 6–9 Months (Exploration & Object Permanence)
- **06** Infant: 9–12 Months (Language & Attachment)
- **07** Toddler: 12–18 Months (Walking & Language Explosion)
- **08** Toddler: 18–24 Months (Emotional Regulation Begins)

### Part 3: The Wonder Years — 2 to 5 (3 modules)
Imagination, empathy, and pre-academic skills take shape.

- **09** Age 2–3: Self-Identity & Parallel Play
- **10** Age 3–4: Imagination & Cooperative Play
- **11** Age 4–5: Pre-Academic Skills & Empathy

### Part 4: School Years & Beyond — 5 to 18 (3 modules)
Supporting continued growth through school, puberty, and adolescence.

- **12** Age 5–7: School Readiness & Friendships
- **13** Age 7–12: Abstract Thinking & Social Navigation
- **14** Age 12–18: Adolescence, Identity & Independence

### Part 5: Cross-Cutting Topics (6 modules)
Essential knowledge that applies across all stages.

- **15** Nutrition & Brain Development
- **16** Sleep Science & Development
- **17** Play-Based Learning
- **18** Screen Time & Technology
- **19** Common Challenges & Solutions
- **20** Resources, FAQ & Further Reading

## Principles

1. **Evidence-based**: Every recommendation grounded in peer-reviewed research
2. **Actionable**: Clear, specific activities — not vague advice
3. **Non-judgmental**: Support all parenting styles without shaming
4. **Culturally inclusive**: Acknowledge diverse family structures and practices
5. **Age-appropriate**: Clear stage markers so parents find relevant content fast
6. **Holistic**: IQ and EQ are inseparable — always address both together

## Key Research Sources

- Harvard Center on the Developing Child
- National Scientific Council on the Developing Child
- American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP)
- World Health Organization (WHO) guidelines
- Developmental psychology peer-reviewed journals (Child Development, Developmental Science)
- Piaget, Vygotsky, Bowlby, Ainsworth, Erikson developmental theories
- Recent neuroimaging studies on infant brain development

## Out of Scope

- Medical diagnosis or treatment of developmental disorders (refer to professionals)
- Specific brand product recommendations
- Formal academic curriculum design
